# Code of conduct

Paralyn welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Discuss the technical work clearly, critique ideas rather than people, and make room for others to participate.

Expected conduct includes respectful language, good-faith review, acknowledgment of uncertainty, credit for others' work, and corrections when evidence changes a conclusion.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. These standards apply to project discussions, reviews, events, and spaces where someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, limit participation, or remove access when necessary. Responses should be proportional to the behavior and consider the safety and privacy of affected people. A maintainer involved in a report should not decide its outcome alone when another uninvolved maintainer is available.

The repository owner is [Jakobthompson15](https://github.com/Jakobthompson15). To arrange a confidential conduct report, open a [minimal contact-request issue](https://github.com/Jakobthompson15/paralyn/issues/new) without incident details or personal information, then agree on a private channel before sharing the report. No monitoring or response-time guarantee is claimed. Do not post another person's private information in an issue.
